Shipping policy

Last updated: October 2026

Where we ship from

All orders are dispatched from the United Kingdom by Ahsan Ventures Ltd.

Processing time

Orders are normally packed and dispatched within 1 to 3 working days of payment. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays. At busy times such as Christmas, processing may take a little longer and we will say so on our listings.

Delivery times

  • United Kingdom: usually 2 to 5 working days after dispatch.
  • Europe: usually 5 to 10 working days after dispatch.
  • Rest of the world: usually 7 to 21 working days after dispatch.

These are estimates. The delivery time confirmed when you order applies to your order.

Shipping costs

Postage is confirmed when you order and depends on the item, the size of the parcel and the destination.

Tracking

Where tracking is available, the number is sent to you by email once your order is dispatched.

Customs and import duties

Orders sent outside the UK may be subject to import duties, VAT or other charges set by the destination country. These are not included in our prices and are the buyer's responsibility.

Lost or damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged or does not arrive, contact us at ahsan4ltd@gmail.com with your order number and, for damage, a photo. We will arrange a replacement or refund.

Returns and refunds

Last updated: October 2026

Your right to cancel

If you are a consumer in the UK, you have the right to cancel your order and return it for any reason within 14 days of receiving it, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. Please tell us you want to cancel within that 14-day period.

How to return an item

  • Email ahsan4ltd@gmail.com with your order number and the item you want to return.
  • We will reply with the return address and instructions.
  • Send the item back within 14 days of telling us you wish to cancel.

Condition of returned items

Items should be unused, in their original packaging and in the condition you received them. You are responsible for taking reasonable care of the goods while they are with you. We may reduce a refund if the item has lost value because it was handled more than needed to check it.

Return postage

Unless the item is faulty or was not what you ordered, the buyer pays the cost of returning it. We recommend using a tracked service, as we cannot be responsible for items lost on the way back to us.

Refunds

Once we receive and check your return, we will refund the price of the item and the original standard postage within 14 days. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If an item is faulty, damaged or not as described, your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 apply. Contact us as soon as possible with photos and we will repair, replace or refund it, and cover the return postage.

Items that cannot be returned

Items made to your specification or clearly personalised, and sealed items that are not suitable for return for hygiene reasons once opened, are excluded from the right to cancel. Any such item will be clearly marked on the listing.

Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your legal rights.
